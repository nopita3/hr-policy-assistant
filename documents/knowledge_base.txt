## Working Hours and Attendance
Standard working hours are from 09:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, including a one-hour lunch break from 12:00 to 13:00. All employees must clock in and clock out every working day using the company attendance app or badge reader. A grace period of 15 minutes is allowed for clock-in; arriving after 09:15 is recorded as late. Three late records in a calendar month may result in a verbal warning from the team lead. Employees who cannot clock in due to a system error must notify HR by email on the same day.

## Annual (Holiday) Leave
Each full-time employee is entitled to 10 working days of annual holiday leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR system at least three working days in advance and require approval from the direct team lead. Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the following year; any balance beyond that is forfeited at year end. Annual leave is fully paid.

## Sick (Medical) Leave
Employees are entitled to up to 30 days of paid medical leave per calendar year. For any absence of three or more consecutive days, a medical certificate from a licensed physician must be submitted to HR. Employees should notify their team lead as early as possible, ideally before the start of the working day. Medical leave cannot be carried over to the next year.

## Business Leave
Business leave covers official, work-related errands that must be handled during working hours, such as renewing a work permit, renewing a driving permit, or attending to government or other official business. Business leave must be requested in advance and requires approval from both the team lead and HR. Supporting documentation (for example, an appointment slip) should be attached to the request. Approved business leave is paid.

## Business and International Travel
All work-related travel, whether domestic or international, must be requested in advance through the HR system and approved by both the team lead and HR; international travel additionally requires department head approval. Travel requests must state the purpose, destination, dates, and estimated cost before any booking is made. For international travel, the employee must hold a passport valid for at least six months and any required visa; HR assists with supporting letters and, for foreign employees, with re-entry or work permit matters. The company books or reimburses economy-class airfare; business class may be approved only for long-haul flights over eight hours with department head approval. A daily per diem covers meals and incidentals, and accommodation is booked by the company or reimbursed against original receipts. The company provides travel insurance for every approved international business trip, covering medical emergencies and trip disruptions. Travel expenses must be submitted within 15 days of returning, following the standard expense reimbursement process. Any personal travel combined with a business trip must be disclosed and approved in advance, and the personal portion is at the employee's own cost.

## Ordination Leave (Lah Buat / ลาบวช)
An employee may apply for ordination leave (ลาบวช) of up to 1 month to enter monkhood. Ordination leave requires approval from both the team lead and HR and should be requested well in advance so that work can be reassigned. Eligibility normally applies to employees who have passed probation. Documentation of the ordination arrangement may be requested by HR.

## Overtime (OT)
Overtime work must be approved in advance by both the team lead and HR before it is performed. Unapproved overtime will not be compensated. Overtime is recorded through the attendance system and compensated in accordance with the applicable labor law rates. Employees should not perform overtime that would compromise health and safety.

## Group Insurance and Medical Benefits
The company provides a group insurance plan to all full-time employees. Outpatient (OPD) treatment is covered up to 2,000 THB per visit (per round). Dental treatment is covered up to 3,500 THB per year. The plan also includes inpatient (IPD) hospitalization coverage under the group policy. Coverage begins after the employee is enrolled by HR; claims are submitted with original receipts through the HR system.

## Payroll and Salary
Salaries are paid once per month on the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, salary is paid on the preceding business day. Payslips are available through the employee self-service portal. Questions about salary calculation, tax deductions, or social security contributions should be directed to HR.

## Probation Period
New employees serve a probation period of 119 days from the start date. During probation, performance is reviewed by the team lead, and either party may end employment with shorter notice as stated in the employment contract. Successful completion of probation confirms full-time status and full benefit eligibility.

## Resignation and Notice Period
An employee who wishes to resign must submit a written resignation letter to their team lead and HR, giving at least 30 days of advance notice. During the notice period, the employee is expected to complete a proper handover of responsibilities. Final pay and any remaining leave settlement are processed in the last payroll cycle.

## Remote Work and Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with prior approval from their team lead. Remote work days must still follow the standard 09:00 to 18:00 hours, and employees must remain reachable during working hours. Roles that require on-site presence may be excluded from the work-from-home arrangement at the manager's discretion.

## Expense Reimbursement
Work-related expenses must be submitted for reimbursement within 15 days of the expense date, with original receipts attached in the HR system. Each claim requires team lead approval. Reimbursements are paid together with the next monthly salary after approval.

## Public Holidays
The company observes the official public holidays announced by the government each year, typically between 13 and 16 days. The annual public holiday calendar is published by HR at the beginning of each year. Public holidays are separate from and additional to annual holiday leave.
